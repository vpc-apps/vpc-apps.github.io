Balloon Domino: privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how Balloon Domino ("the app"), published on Google Play by VPC Apps ("we"), handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, send to our servers, or share any personal information. It works fully offline and needs no account.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (levels unlocked, stars earned) and settings (appearance, sound) only on your device, using Android's standard app storage. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and this version does not use analytics or advertising services.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as advertising or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
