Lantern Chain (browser version): data deletion

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The browser version of Lantern Chain by VPC Apps has no accounts and we keep no player data on any server. Here is how to remove everything the game stores.

Progress saved in your browser

Your progress and sound setting are stored only in your browser. To delete them, clear the site data for the page where you played (website, itch.io or Facebook) in your browser settings, for example in Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site data.

Facebook version

We do not store your Facebook information. To remove the game and the data Meta keeps about your use of it, go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (Instant Games), select Lantern Chain and remove it. Data held by Meta is handled under Meta's own controls and Privacy Policy.

Request deletion from us

If you believe we hold any information about you, email vpconsulting.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Lantern Chain data deletion", saying where you played (website, itch.io or Facebook). We will reply and confirm within 30 days.
