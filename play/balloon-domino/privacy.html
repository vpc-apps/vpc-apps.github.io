Balloon Domino (browser version): privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers the free browser version of Balloon Domino ("the game") made by VPC Apps ("we"), wherever it is played: on our website, on itch.io, on CrazyGames, and as a Facebook Instant Game published with our partner Cognizanceit LLP. The Android app on Google Play has its own policy: Balloon Domino app privacy policy.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking code of our own. On CrazyGames, the platform may show its own ads (see below).
  • We do not run servers for the game and we never receive, sell or share your personal information.
  • The game only remembers your progress and sound setting in your own browser.

Information stored in your browser

The game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars) and the sound on/off setting in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Playing on Facebook

The Facebook version runs inside Facebook through Meta's Instant Games platform. Meta may process information about your use of the game (for example that you played it and, if the game uses them, scores or leaderboards) under Meta's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Facebook name, profile picture, friends list or account ID. We may see only aggregated statistics, such as the number of players.

Playing on CrazyGames

On CrazyGames the game runs inside the CrazyGames website and uses the CrazyGames SDK, which tells CrazyGames when gameplay starts and stops and, when CrazyGames switches ads on for the game, shows video ads (for example an optional ad you choose to watch for a hint or an extra tap). CrazyGames and its advertising partners may process information such as your device, browser and IP address, and may use cookies, under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy; ad and cookie choices are offered by CrazyGames. If you are signed in to CrazyGames, it may also keep a copy of your progress with your account. We do not receive your CrazyGames account details; we may see only aggregated statistics, such as the number of plays.

Playing on itch.io or our website

These sites are hosted by itch.io and GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under their own privacy policies. We do not receive these logs.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

See data deletion instructions.

Changes

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date above.
